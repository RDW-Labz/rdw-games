**What qualifies for a refund at pickup.** A refundable item is one where: (a) the item's condition is substantially worse than described in the listing (not merely that you changed your mind), or (b) the item is materially different from what was listed (e.g., wrong item entirely). **How refunds are handled.** BinGo Auctions staff review the item against the listing description and photos at pickup and make the determination. This on-the-spot review is your remedy for a not-as-described item; a chargeback through your card issuer remains available subject to your card network's rules.

**Chargeback policy.** Chargebacks that reverse payments on valid, completed transactions — meaning the item was picked up and matched the listing — are considered abuse. Accounts with a pattern of abusive chargebacks may be suspended or permanently banned.
