### 2.1 Eligibility requirements

To place bids on BinGo Auctions you must:

- Be a resident of the United States.
- Have the legal capacity to enter into binding contracts under the laws of your state.

### 2.3 Account accuracy

## 3. Bids Are Binding Offers

### 3.1 Nature of a bid

**Every bid you place is a binding, irrevocable offer to purchase.** Placing a bid means you are committing to buy the item at that price if no higher bid is submitted before the auction closes. The auction software displays your maximum bid, manages proxy bidding, and updates the current high bid automatically.

### 3.2 Winning bid creates a contract

When an auction closes and you are the highest bidder, a binding purchase contract is formed between you and BinGo Auctions at the hammer price. You are obligated to pay and to pick up the item.

### 3.3 Payment authorization

By placing a bid, you authorize BinGo Auctions — processing payments through Square — to charge your saved payment method for:

- The winning bid amount (hammer price).
- Any buyer's premium set by BinGo Auctions and disclosed before you bid.
- Applicable sales tax calculated at the time of charge.

Payment is processed at auction close through BinGo Auctions' Square account. BinGo Auctions, through Square, is your merchant of record for the transaction.

### 3.4 No bid retraction

**You cannot retract a bid once placed.** The auction software does not support bid cancellation, and BinGo Auctions staff will not remove a bid at your request. (BinGo Auctions may still withdraw a whole lot; see 3.5.) If you believe you placed a bid in significant error (for example, a genuine technical malfunction, not a change of heart), contact us through the platform immediately; BinGo Auctions has sole discretion whether to accommodate such a request and is under no obligation to do so.

### 3.5 Withdrawn lots

BinGo may withdraw a lot before it closes. Bids on a withdrawn lot are void and no one is charged. If a withdrawn lot was already paid for, it's refunded in full.

### 4.1 When we may require verification

BinGo Auctions may require you to complete identity verification before a bid is accepted or before items are released — for example, for high-value bids or where we suspect fraud — using a third-party verification provider. If verification is required and you decline or do not pass it, the affected bid may be held or rejected and your account may be restricted.

### 4.2 What we receive

Where verification is performed by a third-party provider, that provider processes your identity documents on its own infrastructure under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive only the verification result (verified / not verified / requires review); we do not receive, store, or process copies of your identity documents or facial images.

### 4.5 Refusal to verify

If you decline to complete verification when required, the pending bid will be cancelled and you will not be able to place bids above the threshold until verification is complete. Repeated refusal or submission of fraudulent ID materials is grounds for account suspension.

## 6. Inspect at Pickup; Refunds

### 6.1 Inspect before you accept

Inspect every item at pickup before you accept it. If an item materially differs from what was represented in the listing description or photos in a significant, substantive way, tell our staff at pickup and decline it — we will refund that lot on the spot to your original payment method. Once you accept and take an item, the sale is final and there is no separate post-pickup dispute window.

### 6.2 What qualifies for a refund

A refundable item requires that: (a) the item's condition is substantially worse than described — not merely that it is used, shows age-appropriate wear, or that you have changed your mind; or (b) the item is materially different from what was listed (for example, a completely wrong item was placed in your bin). Minor cosmetic differences visible in the listing photos or consistent with a reasonable reading of the condition notes do not qualify.

### 6.3 How refunds are handled

BinGo Auctions staff review the item against the listing description and photos at pickup and make the determination. Approved refunds are processed through Square back to your original payment method. This on-the-spot review is your remedy for a not-as-described item; a chargeback through your card issuer remains available subject to your card network's rules.

## 7. Pickup-Only Fulfillment

### 7.1 All sales are pickup-only

BinGo Auctions does not offer shipping or delivery unless we explicitly state otherwise in a specific listing. You are responsible for arranging transportation of any item you win.

### 7.2 Pickup window obligations

BinGo Auctions posts a pickup schedule in the listing and in your auction-close notification. You are required to collect your items within that window. If you cannot make the primary pickup window, contact us through the platform as early as possible; accommodations are at our discretion.

### 7.3 No-show forfeiture

**If you do not pick up your item(s) within the posted pickup window and do not make alternate arrangements approved by BinGo Auctions, your payment is forfeited and the item may be relisted.** BinGo Auctions will not issue a refund in connection with a no-show forfeiture. Any per-item no-show fee, if applicable, is disclosed before you bid.

### 7.4 Condition at pickup

Inspect your item at the time of pickup. If you have concerns, note them before leaving the pickup location and initiate the dispute process while you are still on-site if possible. Leaving the pickup location with an item you intend to dispute without documenting the issue contemporaneously may reduce your ability to establish a qualifying dispute.

## 8. Payment Failure

### 8.1 Consequences of failed payment

If your saved payment method is declined at auction close, we will attempt to notify you and provide a brief window to update your payment method. If payment is not successfully collected within that window, BinGo Auctions may cancel the transaction and relist the item at no obligation to you. You will not be entitled to the item and will receive no compensation for any bid increment you contributed.

### 8.2 Effect on account standing

A single payment failure from a declined card does not automatically result in suspension. However, a pattern of payment failures — particularly where items are won and then not paid for — is grounds for account restriction or suspension. Accounts that owe outstanding balances may be restricted from placing new bids until the outstanding amount is resolved.

## 9. Prohibited Bidder Conduct

### 9.1 Shill bidding and collusion

You agree not to engage in shill bidding (bidding on items you have a financial interest in to artificially inflate the price), bid suppression (agreeing with other bidders to keep bids artificially low), or any other form of auction manipulation. These practices are prohibited under platform rules and may violate applicable state auction fraud and consumer protection statutes.

### 9.2 Automated bidding tools

You agree not to use bots, scripts, automated bidding software, or any other automated tool to interact with the bidding system without our prior written consent. Proxy bidding features built into the platform are permitted.

### 9.3 Scraping and data harvesting

You agree not to scrape, crawl, or systematically extract listing data, pricing data, or other platform content using automated means. Accessing the platform through unofficial APIs, reverse-engineered endpoints, or bulk download tools is prohibited.

### 9.4 Prohibited items

You agree not to bid on or facilitate the acquisition of items that appear on the BinGo Auctions Prohibited Items list (Schedule A), available at /legal/prohibited-items. Knowingly bidding on prohibited items is grounds for suspension.

### 9.5 Impersonation and false information

You agree not to impersonate another person, use a false name, or provide false contact information. Winning bidders who cannot be reached for pickup due to false contact information forfeit their items.

### 9.6 Chargeback abuse

Initiating a chargeback with your card issuer for a valid, completed transaction — meaning you collected the item and it matched the listing — constitutes abuse. Accounts with a pattern of abusive chargebacks are subject to suspension, permanent ban, and referral to our fraud detection processes. A chargeback filed in good faith after the dispute process has been exhausted and denied is not abuse.
