## 3. Phase 2 — kept items that were already sold (NOT built; needs Jared/Michael sign-off)

**Covers:** a lot that's closed and won. Either its invoice isn't paid yet, or it's paid (charged).

**Behaviour agreed with Braden:**
- **Won but not yet charged:** take the line off the invoice before the charge. If nothing is left on the invoice, void it. Notify the winner by push + email: "withdrawn, you won't be charged".
- **Charged:** refund the line in full (bid + premium + tax) through the existing item-refund path (`/api/invoices/[id]/refund-items`). Notify by push + email: "withdrawn and refunded $X".
- **Picked up, shipped or disputed:** refuse.

**Must guarantee a withdrawn lot can never be charged:**
- Keep and the charge job both take the **invoice row lock**.
- The auto-charge drain gets a `claim_invoice_for_charge(invoice)` step that re-reads the total without withdrawn lines.
- Keep also cancels the queued `auto_charge_jobs` row.
- **Backstop:** an hourly check. A paid invoice that has a withdrawn line not yet refunded gets an auto-refund and pages a person.

**Also needed:**
- A new `notification_kind` for a withdrawn lot. None exists: prod has 32 kinds, none for withdrawn or canceled.
- **Gap to close:** `remove_lot_from_inventory` (0319) never checks for a won lot or an open invoice. 0381's keep function does refuse those (`lot_on_invoice`).

**Must be proven on the prod copy before shipping** (see §7).
