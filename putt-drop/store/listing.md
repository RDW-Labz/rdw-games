One-thumb mini golf in a warm Pocket Park, played in portrait with a single drag-and-release.

Putt Drop is a hand-crafted mini golf game: drag back from the ball to aim, then release to putt. Every hole is a small,
satisfying puzzle built from walls, bumpers, sand, water, slopes and moving bars. The art is drawn in code — soft sky,
turf islands, trees and flowers — not photo textures.

It runs smoothly, with particles, haptic feedback on every hit, and sound and music generated in the game.
It plays fully offline, with no accounts and no ads. (Buying the full game needs a connection.)

Progress saves on your device. If a hole proves stubborn, an optional assist appears after a few misses.

Features
- One-thumb controls: drag back to aim, release to putt
- Hand-made holes with walls, bumpers, sand, water, slopes and moving bars
- Smooth animation with particles and haptics
- Original sound and music, generated in the game
- Works fully offline
- No ads, no accounts
- Sound and vibration settings
- Progress saved on your device

Free to try: play the first 12 holes. One purchase unlocks 24 more holes. No ads, ever.
