Claim mechanic (https://www.stratussecurity.com/post/aws-subdomain-takeover-guide):
  A regionalized EB hostname `<prefix>.<region>.elasticbeanstalk.com` that returns
  NXDOMAIN is claimable by creating an environment in that same region with that
  DNS name — `aws elasticbeanstalk check-dns-availability --cname-prefix <prefix>`
  then `create-environment --cname-prefix <prefix>`. Hostnames that embed the
  random `<env>.<id>.<region>` segment are still reachable by registering the id;
  legacy non-regional names are not takeable. Ours are the plain regional form.
