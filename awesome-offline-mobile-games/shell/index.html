The rules this collection holds itself to

  • Zero network at play time. Airplane mode is the intended mode. No accounts, no ads, no telemetry, no download-on-demand.
  • Two lanes, labelled. Ports are open-source games rebuilt from pinned commits and referenced by provenance, not vendored. Originals and cross-hybrids are authored in this repo, hybridising the ports' mechanics and running on their real art, audio and game data — 347 files vendored and SHA-256-checked on every test run, now across seven upstream repos.
  • Deep, not tiny. The floor for a slot is "dozens of hours or a real skill ceiling" — no filler to hit ten.
  • Forkable forever. Clone the folder, open it, play it. There is no service to keep alive behind it.

Scope decisions are recorded in docs/SCOPE-GATE.md; the reuse map is docs/HYBRIDIZATION.md; what was actually in the source repo is docs/INVENTORY.md.
