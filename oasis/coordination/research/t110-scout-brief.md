# T110 scout brief — AI desktop/chat UIs to study

Goal: pick 10-15 open-source or public AI desktop/chat apps whose UI is worth studying for OASIS (a Tauri desktop "rig" where non-technical staff chat with agent coworkers that run skills, show approval cards, take over a browser tab, keep tasks and drawers). openhuman first.

For each pick: name, url (repo and/or product site), licence as read from the repo LICENSE file (or "closed/public product"), why it is worth studying (layout, navigation, motion, approval/agent-progress UI, onboarding), and the 1-3 public pages where its UI is best seen in screenshots (README images, docs, product site).

Rules: ideas and layouts only — we copy no code. GPL/AGPL is fine to study, flag it "study only". Prefer apps with agent/tool-run progress, approval prompts, multi-agent or coworker views, desktop chrome. Mix: ~8 open-source, ~4 public closed products. No accounts, no logins, no downloads.
