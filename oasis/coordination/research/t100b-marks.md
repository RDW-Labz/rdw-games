## 1. Official brand/press-kit page + mark file URL
Anthropic: trademark guidelines at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines; press kit download (zip, not opened) at https://www.anthropic.com/press-kit. No public standalone SVG URL — Anthropic states it supplies the image directly on approved request. Deeper portal brand.anthropic.com is email-login-gated (not accessible read-only). || Meta/Llama: Llama license pages at https://www.llama.com/llama4/license/ and https://www.llama.com/llama3_2/license/; general Meta brand resources at https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/meta/company-brand/ (redirect target of about.meta.com). No distinct owner-hosted Llama icon/SVG URL was found; llama.com/ai.meta.com/dev.meta.ai redirects did not serve a Llama-specific logo asset.

## 2. Usage terms governing the mark
Anthropic: "You may only use our trademarks as specifically permitted by us and only in materials we approve beforehand." "We will supply an image... and specific requirements regarding size, pixels, spacing." "No alterations... (changes to color, font, proportion, or otherwise) are permitted." No stated nominative-use carve-out for naming a model in third-party UI. || Meta/Llama: "...you shall prominently display 'Built with Meta Llama' on a related website, user interface, blogpost, about page, or product documentation" (text attribution, required when distributing/using Llama Materials). "...neither Meta nor Licensee may use any name or mark owned by the other except as required for reasonable and customary use in describing and redistributing the Llama Materials." Referred Meta brand page: minimum size 12px/5mm, clear space = 2x symbol height, use primary color variation whenever possible; "all usage of the Meta logo requires approval."

## 3. Verdict per mark
Claude/Anthropic mark: not allowed as a self-serve download-and-use icon — allowed with conditions: Anthropic must pre-approve the specific use and supply the image file (contact marketing@anthropic.com); no alteration; must not imply sponsorship/endorsement/affiliation. || Llama/Meta mark: unverified for a graphical icon (no owner-published standalone Llama SVG/usage terms found) — allowed with conditions for TEXT attribution only: display "Built with Meta Llama" per the license; substituting the generic Meta corporate logo graphic is not allowed without separate approval per Meta's brand page.

## simpleicons.org note
simpleicons.org/simple-icons lists slugs for 'claude', 'anthropic', 'meta', and 'metaai' (no 'llama' slug). Its icon artwork is CC0-licensed, but its own DISCLAIMER.md states this does not waive any trademark rights in the underlying brand and instructs users to seek the brand owner's permission — so owner terms above still govern and simpleicons cannot be used as a substitute license.
